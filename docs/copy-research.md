# Homepage copy notes

Reviewed October 30, 2026. The homepage addresses practical starting points rather than promising transformations or inventing client outcomes.

- [NIH/NIDDK: Tips for Starting Physical Activity](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/tips-get-active/tips-starting-physical-activity): discusses limited time, starting gradually, enjoyable activities, and specific goals scheduled into a routine. These inform the busy-week and returning-to-training copy. These are common barriers discussed in guidance, not measured claims about Kyzen clients.
- [CDC: Adult Activity Overview](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html): general adult guidance includes 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity (or 75 minutes vigorous, or an equivalent combination) plus muscle strengthening on at least two days weekly. Some activity is better than none, and activity can be accumulated throughout the week. The on-page educational panel links directly to this source.

The activity panel is general public-health context, not an individualized exercise prescription. There are no guaranteed results, invented credentials, client statistics, testimonials, or weight-loss timelines. The handstand is identified as an advanced demonstration; beginner visitors are explicitly invited to start at their own level.

The handstand uses the supplied IMG_3513.JPG, normalized for EXIF orientation and resized to an optimized JPEG. CSS controls display brightness, saturation, and contrast while preserving the original photo.
